AI-Powered Decision Support for Disaster Preparedness

RESQOS helps authorities transform heavy-rain alerts into location-specific risk information, emergency-resource awareness and preparedness recommendations.

Explore RESQOS View Dashboard

Built for heavy-rain preparedness in Uttarakhand.

Why RESQOS?

Weather alerts can warn about hazardous rainfall, but authorities still need location-specific information about risk, nearby emergency resources and the actions to prepare. RESQOS brings these together in one decision-support view.

Features

Four capabilities designed to support preparedness decisions before heavy rain arrives.

How RESQOS Works

A six-step workflow from incoming weather information to a clear view for decision-makers.

    Intended Users

    RESQOS is designed to support the teams who plan and coordinate heavy-rain response.
